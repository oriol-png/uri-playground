Methodology & sources

How the score works

Each answer earns points on one of five dimensions — Breadth, Frequency & integration, Technical depth, Agentic & automation, and Leverage & influence. Dimensions are weighted (depth and agentic count most, because they separate the frontier from regular users) into a single 0–100 score. Longer paths sample more dimensions and resolve the top end more precisely.

How percentiles are estimated

There is no clean public dataset of "AI capability percentile," so each population is modeled as a logistic distribution of capability scores. The median of each distribution is anchored to published adoption research (below): the general population skews very low, knowledge workers higher, tech workers higher still, and your function sits where its measured adoption sits. Your score is placed on each curve. Percentiles are bounded to 0.1–99.9 and are directional estimates, not measurements.

Sources used for calibration
  • Pew Research Center (2025) — 34% of US adults have used ChatGPT; ~52% of postgraduates.
  • Gallup Workforce (fall 2025) — ~12% of employed adults use AI daily, ~25% at least weekly; ~60% of tech workers use it frequently.
  • McKinsey, State of AI (2025) — 79% org-level gen-AI use; <10% scaling agents.
  • Stack Overflow Developer Survey (2025) — ~51% of professional developers use AI tools daily.
  • Anthropic Economic Index (2025) — AI use concentrated in computer/math, analytical and writing-heavy occupations.
  • Menlo Ventures / McKinsey — AI spend concentrated in coding (~55%), then IT, marketing, CS, design.
