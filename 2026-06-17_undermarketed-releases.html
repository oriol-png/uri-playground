The fix

📣 A monthly “product drop” on LinkedIn

One carousel or 30-sec demo a month, finance-outcome first. Low production cost, compounding reach. It monetizes the releases that don't justify a press moment but absolutely justify a post — and the same assets double as sales enablement. Start by resurfacing the three below.
